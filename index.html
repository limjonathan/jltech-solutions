IDENTITY

Identity & Access Management

Zero-trust identity architectures that keep the right people in and the wrong people out.

  • Active Directory — Forest consolidation, group policy optimization, hybrid sync with Azure AD.
  • Azure AD / Entra ID — Cloud identity, SSO, conditional access, and MFA deployment.
  • Okta & Third-Party IdP — Multi-vendor identity federation and SAML/OIDC integration.

  • MFA Rollouts — Enterprise-wide multi-factor authentication with user training and support.
  • Privileged Access Management — Admin tier isolation, just-in-time access, audit logging.
